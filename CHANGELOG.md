# Changelog

Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`. Before public release the major number stays at 0.

- **PATCH** (0.2.0 → 0.2.1): points or stat changes, typo and name fixes, small bug fixes.
- **MINOR** (0.2.x → 0.3.0): new armies, units, command cards or builder features.
- **MAJOR** (0.x → 1.0.0): public release, or a rules change that breaks old lists.

## 0.5.3 — 2026-10-23

### Command cards
- New faction terrain cards, 1 per deck: **Prepared Ground** (Persia), **Phocian Wall** (Greece) and **Chinampas** (Aztecs).

## 0.5.2 — 2026-10-23

### Command cards
- New Roman terrain cards, 1 per deck: **Fossa** (a flank trench mounted units can't cross) and **Līlia** (pit traps that wound and shake Cavalry, Chariots and Elephants).

## 0.5.1 — 2026-10-23

### Command cards
- Bigger terrain: Dense Brush 6” × 6”, Crumbled Ruins 8” × 8”, Flooded Field 6” × 6”.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-10-23

### Command cards
- New generic terrain cards, open to every army and up to 3 per deck: **Monument**, **Watchtower**, **Dense Brush**, **Crumbled Ruins** and **Flooded Field**. Sizes and limits are playtest values.

## 0.4.4 — 2026-10-23

### Rules
- Phalanx is negated only if the unit has neither a Shield nor a weapon of Length 6 or more (was: greater than 6).

### Command cards
- New Aztec card: **Death Whistle**. Enemy units within 24” of a unit with a Shaman or Army Musician take a Fear Test at -1; those that fail lose their Hold Action Token.
- Fulcum: dropped the Phalanx requirement waiver, which the new Phalanx wording makes unnecessary.

## 0.4.3 — 2026-10-22

### Command cards
- Fulcum: units may also use their Pīlum in Melee as in Testūdō (Length 6, -1 Strength, no Sunder), even without the Hold action.

## 0.4.2 — 2026-10-22

### Command cards
- New Hittite card: **Kadesh Ambush**. One Chariot unit gains Scout; you lose Army Morale equal to half of what that unit adds to your Starting Army Morale (rounded up). The penalty is a playtest value.

## 0.4.1 — 2026-10-22

### Command cards
- Supplemental Mercenaries: up to 3 per deck. Each copy allows 1 mercenary unit.
- Triplex Aciēs can be played on a unit that is Locked in Combat.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-22

### Command cards
- New faction cards, each with rules text and flavor text:
  - Persia: Blot Out the Sun, All of Mankind, Scythicon Poison (plus King of Kings)
  - Hittites: Chariots of the Storm God, The Thousand Gods of Hatti
  - Rome: Triplex Aciēs, Impetus, Fulcum (plus Numerī)
  - Aztecs: Flower War, Blood for Huītzilōpōchtli
  - Greece: Othismos, In the Shade
- Every card shows how many copies a deck may hold. Faction and mercenary cards are 1 per deck; generic cards can allow up to 3.
- Supplemental Mercenaries gains flavor text.

### Builder
- Cards allowed more than once get a − / + counter, and the list sheet shows copies as "×2".

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-22

### Command cards

- New Rome-only card: **Numerī**. Take 2 mercenary units, chosen from Cataphracts, Numidian Javelineers, Arabian Camel Riders and Thracian Warriors.

### Builder
- Faction Command cards only appear for their own army.
- Mercenary units are checked against the cards in your deck: each unit must fit a card that allows it, and the mercenary list only shows units your deck can take.

### Mercenaries
- Nabatean Skirmishers renamed **Arabian Camel Riders**. Saved lists still load.
