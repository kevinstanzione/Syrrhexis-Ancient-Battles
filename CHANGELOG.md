# Changelog

Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`. Before public release the major number stays at 0.

- **PATCH** (0.2.0 → 0.2.1): points or stat changes, typo and name fixes, small bug fixes.
- **MINOR** (0.2.x → 0.3.0): new armies, units, command cards or builder features.
- **MAJOR** (0.x → 1.0.0): public release, or a rules change that breaks old lists.

## 0.2.4 — 2026-10-22

### Rules
- New armor: **Barding** (horse armor, Cavalry only). +1 Armor, -1 Move. Stacks with the rider's armor, shield and the Cavalry Toughness bonus.

### Persia
- Asabāra: Barding option, +2 points per model.
- Asabāra Retinue: Barding option, +3 points per model.
- Asabāra and Asabāra Retinue: removed the stale "reduced length" note from the Arštaya.

### Mercenaries
- Cataphract: Barding by default (Armor 5, 2+; Move 8). 25 → 28 points.

## 0.2.3 — 2026-10-21

### Weapon lengths
- Javelins used in melee are Length 6 everywhere, matching the mercenary and Hittite versions: Greek Akóntion (Peltastaí, Psiloí), Persian Arštaya (Kardakā, Takabāra, Sakā, Anušiya, Asabāra, Asabāra Retinue), Roman Lancea (Auxilia, Cohors Equitāta, Explōrātor). Chariot and Pīrū Arštaya unchanged.
- Hasta 7 → 8, level with the Persian Aršti (Auxilia, Cohors Equitāta, Cohors Equitāta Retinue).
- Hittite GIŠ.TUKUL 2 → 3, level with the other clubs.

### Mercenaries
- Cataphract: Contus 10 → 11, so it strikes before the Dóry.
- Lydian Houndsmen: Dóry 9 → 10, matching every other Dóry. 15 → 16 points.

### To review
- Mācuahuitl Length 4 → 5? Real examples ran about 70–100 cm, closer to a spatha.

## 0.2.2 — 2026-10-20

### Mercenaries
- Carduchian Tribesmen: Aġeġn bow gains Unwieldy (1) and Sunder (1). 11 → 13 points.

### Builder
- Open Order units now show the Unwieldy (X) Move penalty (e.g. Carduchians with the Aġeġn move 6).

## 0.2.1 — 2026-10-20

### Rules
- Recurve Bow: Direct Fire now applies within half of maximum range instead of a quarter. It no longer ignores the "Attacker moved" penalty.
- Unwieldy (X): Open Order units suffer -X Move instead of the extra turning cost.

### Hittites
- Hattian Archers and Hattian Chariots lose Recurve Bow (keep Direct Fire). Allied Archers and Allied Chariots keep it.
- Hattian Archers 11 → 9 points.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-20

### Builder
- Weapon descriptions under native names (e.g. Macuahuitl, "obsidian-edged sword"), also in the text export.
- Hover/tap tooltips for rule keywords.
- Command deck picker (6 cards). First card: Supplemental Mercenaries, which unlocks 1 mercenary unit.
- Supplemental Mercenaries roster (13 units).
- Camel Riders rule: extra riders count toward Army Morale and Unit Strength.
- Version number shown on the page, the army list and the text export.

### Rules and points
- Sunder (X) keyword replaces "enemies roll Armor Saves at -2".
- Army Standard Bearer 28 (Cohors Equitata Retinue 25); Army Musician 60.
- Takabāra gain Houndmaster [10]. Wall of Spara moves to the Spara shield.
- Tribal Leaders and Lords repriced; Greek Peltastaí take Lead Captain [5] or Raider Captain [8].
- Chariots up about 25%: Hattian 75, Allied 50, Raθa 50, Raθa-Dāça 48, Raθa Retinue 90.

### Names
- Hittite: GÍR.GAL.GUR, ḪA.ZI.IN. Persia: Raθa-Dāça. Aztec: Tlahhuītōlli.
- Latin macrons and Greek accents made consistent.
- Native weapon names for all mercenary units.
