# Changelog

Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`. Before public release the major number stays at 0.

- **PATCH** (0.2.0 → 0.2.1): points or stat changes, typo and name fixes, small bug fixes.
- **MINOR** (0.2.x → 0.3.0): new armies, units, command cards or builder features.
- **MAJOR** (0.x → 1.0.0): public release, or a rules change that breaks old lists.

## 0.7.1 — 2026-10-23

### Command cards
- New generic card: **Vanguard** (up to 3 per deck). During deployment, one light or skirmishing foot or cavalry unit gains Scout.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-10-23

### Rules
- **Stubborn** renamed **Disciplined**. It also automatically passes the special Break Test from Feign Retreat (it may still choose to be goaded).

### Command cards
- New generic card: **Last Stand** (up to 3 per deck). For one turn, all your units gain Suicidal.
- Local Guides now affects up to two units.
- Hold the Line grants Disciplined.

## 0.6.9 — 2026-10-23

### Command cards
- New generic cards, up to 3 per deck: **Mass Volley** (one ranged unit gains Volley), **Hold the Line** (all your Closed Order units gain Stubborn), **Local Guides** (one foot unit gains Surefooted) and **Hidden Ravine** (one Light or Skirmishing Warrior unit gains Ambush).

## 0.6.8 — 2026-10-23

### Rules
- Frenzy trimmed: keeps its Charge token against bigger chargers, ignores Terrify, and gets +2 to Break Tests and Fear Tests. It no longer gives +1 to self-Rally, and the reference to a Run token Accuracy penalty is gone.

## 0.6.7 — 2026-10-23

### Command cards
- New generic card: **Bodyguard** (up to 3 per deck). For one round, a foot unit with base Training 7+ that isn't a Retinue gains Guard.

## 0.6.6 — 2026-10-23

### Rules
- Volley reworded: -1 Accuracy and -1 Strength, and the extra Army Morale loss is half the number of Volley attacks, rounded down.

## 0.6.5 — 2026-10-23

### Characters
- Houndmaster: the charge drain uses the unit's base Training. Its cost is now Training + 5: 10 on Training 5 units, 13 on Lēgiōnārius Retinue and Praetōriānī Retinue.

## 0.6.4 — 2026-10-23

### Command cards
- The Hot Gates: a Phalanx unit gains +2 Move instead of +1.

## 0.6.3 — 2026-10-23

### Command cards
- New generic card: **Feigned Flight** (up to 3 per deck). For one turn, up to two Light Cavalry units with Feign Charge also gain Feign Retreat.
- New Greek card: **The Hot Gates** (1 per deck). For one turn, one infantry unit gains Feign Retreat and +1 Move.

## 0.6.2 — 2026-10-23

### Persia
- Asagartiyā and Sakā: 2 Ranks of 4 or 2 Ranks of 3 Cavalry (no more single-rank option for these Levy units).

### Rules
- Feign Retreat drains as much Army Morale as Feign Charge (1 per warrior, 3 per cavalry), so it is strictly stronger.
- Feign Retreat wording: a failed special Break Test means the target is goaded; a passed test lets it choose to be. A goaded unit that has already activated this round suffers no further restriction.

## 0.6.1 — 2026-10-23

### Persia
- Asagartiyā: Birdmaster option for 8.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-23

### Characters
- **Raider Captain** replaced by **Skirmish Captain**. It no longer grants Feign Charge or free movement through allies. Instead, when charged, the unit may take a Fear Test to fall back through allied units, and the charger may carry on into the unit behind. Light Warriors with one still deploy in Open Order.
- Asagartiyā lose the captain option (they keep Feign Charge as a unit rule).
