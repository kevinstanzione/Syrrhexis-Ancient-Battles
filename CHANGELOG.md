# Changelog

Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`. Before public release the major number stays at 0.

- **PATCH** (0.2.0 → 0.2.1): points or stat changes, typo and name fixes, small bug fixes.
- **MINOR** (0.2.x → 0.3.0): new armies, units, command cards or builder features.
- **MAJOR** (0.x → 1.0.0): public release, or a rules change that breaks old lists.

## 0.32.1 — 2026-11-13

### Unit descriptions
- Reworded: Eren (shieldbearers and spearmen), Šub-lugal (studded cape spearmen), Guruš (simple levy warriors), Pḥrrw (chariot support), Snnyw and Wrrywt (chariot archers), Ḏꜣmw (levy recruits), Ssmtyw (javelin-armed horsemen), Sparabārā (shieldbearers and archers), Kardakā (armored levy infantry), Takabārā (light levy infantry), Hoplîtai Retinue (the general’s hoplites), Sphendonêtai (expert slingers), Thureophóroi (medium infantry with spears and javelins), Oxybeleîs (light bolt-thrower), Lēgiōnāriī Retinue (the legate’s personal legionaries), Explōrātōrēs (long-range scouts), Nam.Ra (resettled captive levy).

### Names
- Ichcahuipilli is now spelled Ichcahuīpīlli (both i's in huīpīlli are long).
- War machine weapons (Oxybelḗs, Scorpiō Bolt, Carroballista Bolt) are described by their projectile instead of the machine: "heavy bolt" for the Oxybelḗs, "iron-headed bolt" for the Roman engines.
- The Greek Sphendónē is described as a "lead-bullet sling".

### Army Book layout
- A unit's pronunciation and description stay together: beside the name when the whole line fits (measured from the actual fonts), otherwise both on their own line between the name and the unit type. Long names such as Šukur.Tur Kaška no longer wrap.
- That line is now a warm brown, distinct from the gray unit type (builder too).

## Rulebook — 2v2 (2026-11-05)

- 2v2 Games rewritten: a team plays as one side; 4’ × 6’ board with a shared Friendly edge and deployment zone; team bid is the sum of both players' bids (up to 50); each player keeps their own Command deck and hand, but card effects apply to the team; the team deploys as one army (shared Byes, First Player picks who deploys first and who places Scouts and Ambushers first); one Initiative roll per team with both teammates' bonuses; shared Army Morale. ## Rulebook — 2026-11-05

- Frenzy: no longer gives +2 on Break Tests and Fear Tests. It still ignores Terrify Break Tokens and keeps its Charge token when charged by a bigger unit.
- Melees: every unit joined through base contact with an enemy is one Melee, so chained fights merge. Units attack only enemies they touch and may split attacks between them; Damage is totalled per side, with one winner and one Combat Move. A Melee that splits becomes separate Melees.
- Combat Moves: a moving unit stops at an enemy, a friendly unit, impassable terrain or the board edge, so it may move less than 2” or not at all; still in contact means still Locked in Combat. A Pushed unit that cannot move at least 1” gains 2 Break Tokens.

## 0.16.0 — 2026-11-04

### Rules
- Aim now reduces Cover by one step, like Volley (was: ignores Light Cover).
- Defending a Wall: a unit may charge an enemy anywhere behind a wall that it could reach without the wall. The charger stops at the wall and the defender is drawn up into contact with it (up to its Move; if it can't reach, the Charge fails). Closes the gap where a unit standing just behind a wall couldn't be charged.
- Marsh: every Break Token effect now gives 2 Break Tokens (moving within it, an Elephant starting wholly within it, and a stuck Chariot or Warmachine, which was 4).
- Stony Plain: Chariots, Elephants and Warmachines take 1 hit at Strength 6 for each Chariot, Elephant or war engine (was 1 hit at Strength 4).
- Gain X and Lose X on the Hitting and Wounding tables now add or remove dice instead of successes: Gain X rolls X extra dice, Lose X rolls X fewer (a pool can reach 0). This softens low Accuracy and Strength and tames the flat bonus at 7 and above.

### Special Characters
- Characters no longer add flat amounts of Army Morale (Birdmaster, Bowmaster, Houndmaster, Musician, Ranger Captain, Shaman, Skirmish Captain, Tribal Leader, Volleymaster). Characters whose bonus scales with Training keep it; the Musician now adds its Training (was a flat +3). Taskmaster's "no Army Morale" note removed.
- Birdmaster: if its unit deploys via Scout or Ambush, the opponent reveals 2 random unpicked Command cards (was 1, Scout only).
- Houndmaster: when its unit's Charge makes contact, the defender loses Army Morale equal to the unit's base Training (reworded for current rules).
- Rear Captain: +1 to Break Tests and to Rally itself (was +2).
- Commander and Army Standard Bearer: Army Morale lost while their unit flees, and when it is eliminated, is now their Training (was 2×), the same as the Lord, Standard Bearer and Army Musician.

### Deployment
- The First Player chooses who deploys first, and also chooses who deploys their Scouting units first and who deploys their Ambushing units first (was decided by Byes, with a roll-off).

### 2v2
- Teams share one Army Morale: both armies' Starting Army Morale is added together, and gains, losses and categories apply to the shared total. "Each opponent gains" Army Morale (Lord, Standard Bearers, Musicians, Commander) is now "the opponent", meaning the opposing team in 2v2.

### Points (re-priced for the new tables)
- Pīrū 100 → 90; Raθā-Dāça 50 → 46; Scorpiō engine 60 → 50; Carroballista engine 70 → 62; Thracian Warriors 12 → 11.
- Guruš 7 → 8; Martu 11 → 12; Lydian Houndsmen 16 → 17.
