Introduction

This game is a work-in-progress system for miniature war gaming in the Bronze Age through the end of Classical Antiquity. This is not a simulation for recreating massive battles from history, but an anachronistic and ahistorical miniature game meant for smashing together units from different eras and locales. The goal of this game is to utilize the large number of historical figures designed for collectors, to create a fun and exciting miniature game that has meaningful and tactical depth. This game is clearly inspired by Games Workshop’s Warhammer Ancient Battles game, but seeks to take tray-based miniature wargaming a step further, using inspiration from a plethora of popular miniature and board games.

Update History

Version 0.0.1 - February 2026: first barebones version.

Version 0.1.0 - October 2026

Version 0.2.0 - November 2026: chapters reordered so that units, army building and setting up come before the rules of play, and the terrain rules gathered into a Terrain chapter. Rules changes include Line of Fire and Cover, Walls, Feign Charge and Feign Retreat, Breaking, Gain and Lose on the Hitting and Wounding tables now adding or removing dice, Aim reducing Cover by one step, the Shooting Modifiers named and reorganized, simpler Special Character Army Morale bonuses, what counts as one Melee, Combat Moves against units with nowhere to go, and a simpler Frenzy.

Version 0.3.0 - November 2026: rules review. Army Morale is now lost per unit Contribution (25% for each Health category entered, each failed Break Test and each Fleeing Step) instead of per point of Damage, and mounts add Army Morale (1 per horse, donkey or camel, 5 per elephant). New rules: About Face and forward-only movement for Closed Order units, Splitting a Unit, Blocked Flee moves, Goading, Attacks with Multiple Weapons, and Building a Command deck. Rules changes include optional Scout and Ambush (Ambushers deploy more than 1” from the enemy), fleeing units moving only in the Fleeing Step, on-Charge weapons needing a Charge Action Token, the Army Musician not affecting Chariots or Elephants, Volley adding half range instead of doubling it, Gain and Lose applying once per attack and never to Warmachine hit rolls, a natural 1 always failing Break and Fear Tests, and many clarifications and wording fixes.

Version 0.3.1 - November 2026: Ongoing and terrain Command cards stay face up with the player who played them, units cannot Charge in the round they arrive from Reserve, and armor and shield Move penalties do not apply to Chariot or Elephant crews.

Version 0.3.2 - November 2026: Elephants gain Momentum, and Warmachines never Gain or Lose dice on the Wounding Table as well as the Hitting Table. Warmachine profiles show the war engine’s cost separately from the Engineers’ stats.

Version 0.3.3 - November 2026: a weapon’s abilities apply only to attacks made with it, unless the ability applies to a unit equipped with the weapon, such as Unwieldy (X).

Version 0.3.4 - November 2026: pencil illustrations added to the Movement, Shooting, Melee and Special Characters chapters. No rules changes.
