Building an Army

List Making

To build an army, first choose one of the Army Lists. Then select units from that list, following its restrictions on units, up to the agreed points value. Standard games are 2000 points per side, or 1250 points per player in 2v2 games. Each player also builds a Command deck of 6 cards (see Commands).

Every model in a unit must have the same Loadout unless its profile says otherwise (see Gear, Equipment, and Upgrades). Outside of special scenarios, an army may include only one of each Retinue Special Character. Units from the Supplemental Mercenaries list can only be added to an army through Command cards.

Trays, Scale, and Representation

Syrrhēxis is designed for 1/72 scale miniatures, using the base sizes listed in Physical Aspects of a Model.

Trays

Closed Order units are placed on trays sized to hold their models in the ranks and files of their Unit Composition. Because trays from different makers vary, a tray may have a border of up to ¼” (6mm) on each side. Movement and charges are measured from the edge of the tray, as described in Movement. A tray has four edges: its front edge is the one its models face, along its first rank; the opposite edge is its rear edge; and the two edges between them are its left and right flank edges. A unit's flanks and rear are these edges, and they matter for charges, Exposed Flanks, and Collision.

Bases

Closed Order models use the square and rectangular bases listed in Physical Aspects of a Model. Open Order models may use those bases, or round or oval bases instead. A round or oval base must be at least as wide as the listed base, and no longer than the listed base’s diagonal. For example, an Open Order Warrior may use a round base from 20mm to 28mm across.

Representation

Each model should show the Equipment on its profile, so that both players can tell units apart at a glance. Gear does not need to be shown. Special Character models do not need to show their Equipment, but should be clearly recognizable as Special Characters, for example with a distinct pose, banner, or instrument.
