Initiative

After players have placed all their Order Tokens, they proceed to the beginning of the Activation Phase and determine the order of Initiative for the round. Each player rolls 1d6, adding any bonuses they may have from command cards or effects. If players tie, the tied players roll again, adding the same bonuses, until the tie is broken.

The result, from highest to lowest, is the Initiative order. Whenever a rule says to act in Initiative order, use the most recent Initiative roll: the Start of the Round, Reserves and Orders Phases use the previous round’s roll, while the Activation, Combat and Morale Phases use this round’s. In the first round, before Initiative has been rolled, the First Player goes first.
