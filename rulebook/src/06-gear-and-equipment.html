Gear and Equipment

This section covers how armor and shields protect a model. For the difference between Equipment and Gear, see Gear, Equipment, and Upgrades.

Armor Value

A model’s Armor Value is the sum of its Body Armor, its Shield, its Barding, and its Unit Type’s Armor Modifier (see Unit Types). Elephants are the exception: their Armor Value is always 3 (see Great Toughness). Armor Value is always checked at the moment an attack is made, using every bonus and penalty in effect at that time, such as a Shield’s bonus while the unit has a Hold Action Token, or a Heavy Shield’s penalty while the unit has a Run or Flee Action Token. Weapon special rules that check the target’s Armor, such as Cleaving Blade (X), use this value.

Some armor and shields change their Armor bonus depending on the Strength of the attack, such as the Persian Spara and the Aztec Chīmalli and Ichcahuipilli, which give 1 less Armor against weapons of Strength 6 or greater. These always check the attacking weapon’s profile Strength, ignoring any bonus from Cleaving Blade (X) and all other Strength modifiers.
