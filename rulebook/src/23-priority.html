Priority and Ability Interactions

When several abilities or effects would happen at the same time, the active player chooses the order they resolve in. During a unit’s activation, the player activating that unit is the active player. Outside of a unit’s activation, the player or team that is first in Initiative order chooses the order.

Stacking Values. When a unit, model or weapon has the same special rule with a number (X) from more than one source, add the numbers together. For example, a weapon with Unwieldy (1), used by a unit that also has Unwieldy (1) from another source, has Unwieldy (2). Rules without a number, such as Disciplined, do not stack: having one twice is the same as having it once.
