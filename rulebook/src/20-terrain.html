Terrain

These rules cover the Major Terrain chosen during Setting Up and the terrain features that Command cards place on the battlefield.

Difficult Terrain

While a unit is within Difficult Terrain:

  • It does not gain +1 Strength from a Charge Action Token
  • It does not gain +1 Accuracy from a Hold Action Token

Major Terrain Types

Each type of Major Terrain brings its own hardships, and some troops suffer far more than others.

Stony Plain

Large rocks dot the windswept landscape, making traversal difficult for most.

  • Difficult Terrain
  • If any part of a unit’s move enters or crosses a Stony Plain, its Move Allowance for that whole move cannot exceed its Move Value, so a Run or Charge covers no more ground than a Standard Move. Skirmishing Warrior units are exempt
  • Chariots, Elephants, and Warmachines gain a Break Token and suffer 1 hit at Strength 6 for each Chariot, Elephant, or war engine in the unit every time any movement has them move within a Stony Plain
  • The following abilities are negated while within a Stony Plain:
    • Feign Charge
    • Feign Retreat
    • Phalanx
    • Shieldwall
    • Pavise
    • Reach
    • Collision

Marsh

Swampy wetlands bog down all soldiers, and stop wheeled vehicles dead.

  • Difficult Terrain
  • If any part of a unit’s move enters or crosses a Marsh, its Move Allowance for that whole move cannot exceed its Move Value, so a Run or Charge covers no more ground than a Standard Move. This applies even to Skirmishing Warrior units
  • Chariots, Elephants, and Warmachines gain a Break Token every time any movement has them move within a Marsh
  • If an Elephant unit begins its activation wholly within a Marsh, it gains a Break Token and suffers a 50% movement penalty
  • If a Chariot or Warmachine unit begins its activation wholly within a Marsh, it gains 4 Break Tokens. It cannot move unless it is in base contact with a Closed Order unit with greater Unit Strength, in which case it suffers a 50% movement penalty instead
  • The following abilities are negated while within a Marsh:
    • Feign Charge
    • Feign Retreat
    • Phalanx
    • Shieldwall
    • Testūdō
    • Pavise
    • Reach
    • Collision

Sand

The shifting sand beneath the warriors’ feet makes it difficult to gather much momentum or stand your ground.

  • Difficult Terrain

Terrain Features

All terrain features other than the Major Terrain are placed on the battlefield by Command cards. See Commands. Some of these cards are marked Obstacle: structures and growth that a determined crew can tear down, fill in or clear, such as walls, trenches, traps, towers, monuments and brush. Effects such as Sappers can cancel an Obstacle card before its feature is placed. Terrain cards that change the ground itself, such as Crumbled Ruins, Flooded Fields, Chinampas and Prepared Ground, are not Obstacles and cannot be cancelled this way. Some Obstacles are also marked Flammable, such as wooden towers, brush and wagons; effects such as Fire Brands can burn them during the battle. A terrain feature from a Command card affects Line of Sight and Cover only as its card says. If the card does not say, the feature does not block Line of Sight and gives no Cover, whatever model is used to represent it.

Walls. Some Obstacles are also marked Wall, such as the Field Wall and the Phocian Wall. Unlike other Obstacles, a wall can be crossed, shot over and defended.

Crossing a Wall. A Warrior or Cavalry unit may cross a wall during a Standard Move or Run. Its Move Allowance for that move is halved, and it must end the move entirely clear of the wall. A unit cannot cross a wall during a Charge, Flee or Disengage. Chariots, Elephants and Warmachines cannot cross walls.

Shooting over a Wall. A unit in base contact with a wall may shoot over it: that wall gives its targets no Cover. An Open Order unit’s Leader must be in base contact with the wall.

Defending a Wall. An enemy unit may charge a unit that is in base contact with a wall from the other side. The charging unit stops when it touches the wall, and the two units count as being in base contact and Locked in Combat across it. The wall breaks the Charge: the charging unit gains no +1 Strength from its Charge Action Token, causes no Collision, and cannot use weapons that may only be used on the Charge.
