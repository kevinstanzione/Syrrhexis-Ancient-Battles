Terrain

These rules cover the Major Terrain chosen during Setting Up and the terrain features that Command cards place on the battlefield.

Difficult Terrain

While a unit is within Difficult Terrain:

  • It does not gain +1 Strength from a Charge Action Token
  • It does not gain +1 Accuracy from a Hold Action Token

Major Terrain Types

Each type of Major Terrain brings its own hardships, and some troops suffer far more than others.

Sand

The shifting sand beneath the warriors’ feet makes it difficult to gather much momentum or stand your ground.

  • Difficult Terrain

Stony Plain

Large rocks dot the windswept landscape, making traversal difficult for most.

  • Difficult Terrain
  • If any part of a unit’s move enters or crosses a Stony Plain, its Move Allowance for that whole move cannot exceed its Move Value, so a Run or Charge covers no more ground than a Standard Move. Open Order Warrior units are exempt
  • Chariots, Elephants, and Warmachines gain a Break Token and suffer 1 hit at Strength 6 for each Chariot, Elephant, or war engine in the unit every time any movement has them move within a Stony Plain
  • The following abilities are negated while within a Stony Plain:
    • Feign Charge
    • Feign Retreat
    • Phalanx
    • Shieldwall
    • Pavise
    • Reach
    • Collision
    • Momentum

Marsh

Swampy wetlands bog down all soldiers, and stop wheeled vehicles dead.

  • Difficult Terrain
  • If any part of a unit’s move enters or crosses a Marsh, its Move Allowance for that whole move cannot exceed its Move Value, so a Run or Charge covers no more ground than a Standard Move. This applies even to Skirmishing Warrior units
  • Chariots, Elephants, and Warmachines gain 2 Break Tokens every time any movement has them move within a Marsh
  • If an Elephant unit begins its activation wholly within a Marsh, it gains 2 Break Tokens and suffers a 50% movement penalty
  • If a Chariot or Warmachine unit begins its activation wholly within a Marsh, it gains 2 Break Tokens. It cannot move unless it is in base contact with a Closed Order unit with greater Unit Strength, in which case it suffers a 50% movement penalty instead
  • The following abilities are negated while within a Marsh:
    • Feign Charge
    • Feign Retreat
    • Phalanx
    • Shieldwall
    • Pavise
    • Reach
    • Collision
    • Evasion
    • Momentum

Woodland

Open woods break up the ranks and hide the enemy until he is upon you.

  • Difficult Terrain
  • If any part of a unit’s move enters or crosses a Woodland, its Move Allowance for that whole move cannot exceed its Move Value, so a Run or Charge covers no more ground than a Standard Move. Open Order Warrior units are exempt
  • Units within a Woodland have Light Cover. A line of fire cannot cross more than 6” of Woodland
  • Units within a Woodland cannot use Volley
  • Chariots and Elephants gain 2 Break Tokens every time any movement has them move within a Woodland
  • Warmachines cannot enter a Woodland, and cannot be deployed within one
  • The following abilities are negated while within a Woodland:
    • Feign Charge
    • Feign Retreat
    • Phalanx
    • Shieldwall
    • Pavise
    • Reach
    • Collision
    • Momentum

Terrain Features

All terrain features other than the Major Terrain are placed on the battlefield by Command cards. See Commands. Some of these cards are marked Obstacle: structures and growth that a determined crew can tear down, fill in or clear, such as walls, trenches, traps, towers, monuments and brush. Effects such as Sappers can cancel an Obstacle card before its feature is placed. Terrain cards that change the ground itself, such as Crumbled Ruins, Flooded Fields, Chinampas and Prepared Ground, are not Obstacles and cannot be cancelled this way. Some Obstacles are also marked Flammable, such as wooden towers, brush and wagons; effects such as Fire Brands can burn them during the battle. A terrain feature from a Command card affects lines of fire and Cover only as its card says. If the card does not say, the feature does not block lines of fire and gives no Cover, whatever model is used to represent it.

Placing Terrain. Terrain is placed in layers:

  • The Major Terrain is the bottom layer. Any terrain may be placed over it.
  • Ground terrain (cards that change the ground itself, such as Crumbled Ruins, Flooded Fields, Chinampas, Prepared Ground and the Straight Road) may overlap the Major Terrain and each other. Where they overlap, each card’s own rules decide which applies: for example, Prepared Ground cancels a Flooded Field.
  • Every other terrain feature may be placed on ground terrain, but may never overlap or touch another terrain feature.
  • An impassable terrain feature must be placed at least 1” from every other impassable terrain feature.
  • Features placed or moved during the battle, such as Caltrops, released wagons, pigs and oxen, ignore the 1” rule, but still cannot be placed overlapping another terrain feature.

Walls. Some Obstacles are also marked Wall, such as the Field Wall and the Phocian Wall. Unlike other Obstacles, a wall can be crossed, shot over and defended.

Crossing a Wall. A Warrior or Cavalry unit may cross a wall during a Standard Move or Run. Its Move Allowance for that move is halved, and it must end the move entirely clear of the wall. A unit cannot cross a wall during a Charge, Flee or Disengage. Chariots, Elephants and Warmachines cannot cross walls.

Shooting over a Wall. A unit in base contact with a wall may shoot over it: that wall gives its targets no Cover. An Open Order unit’s Leader must be in base contact with the wall.

Defending a Wall. A unit may declare a Charge against an enemy unit on the other side of a wall if it could reach that enemy were the wall not there. The charging unit stops when it touches the wall. Unless it is already touching the wall, the defending unit is drawn up to defend it: a Closed Order unit moves directly forward, and each model of an Open Order unit moves straight toward the wall, up to its Move value, until it touches the wall. It keeps its Hold Action Token if it has one. If it cannot reach the wall, the Charge fails. The two units then count as being in base contact and Locked in Combat across the wall. The wall breaks the Charge: the charging unit gains no +1 Strength from its Charge Action Token, causes no Collision, and cannot use weapons that may only be used on the Charge.
