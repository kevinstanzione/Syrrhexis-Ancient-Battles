Golden Rules

There are a few Golden Rules that should always be kept in mind when playing Syrrhēxis: Ancient Battles:

  1. No Premeasuring. Unless otherwise stated, at no time may you use any tool, device, or prop to attempt to measure a distance before you would otherwise do so
  2. Only your Cards are Secret. Players must share lists before the match, and thoroughly explain all abilities and equipment prior to the game, and again at any point a player is unsure.
