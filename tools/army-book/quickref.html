Breakpoints are rounded down: a unit with 25 Health breaks at 18, 12, 6 and 0. Penalties are not cumulative; entering a worse category applies only the difference.

Army Morale

Starting Army Morale is the sum of every model's Training. Count Drivers, Mahouts and Crewmen as models; Horses and Elephants do not count. Special Characters add their own bonus.
