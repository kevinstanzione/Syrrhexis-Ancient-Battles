How to Read a Profile

Every unit in this book is printed the same way. This page explains each line.

Header

Move is in inches, before any penalties from armor, shields, Barding or Phalanx. Training is the unit's discipline and morale. Points are per model: per Warrior or Cavalry model, per chariot (with its crew and animals) for Chariots, and per elephant (with its crew) for Elephants.

Warmachines show a war engine cost in brackets, for example “1 Warmachine [50] & 3–6 Engineers”. The unit costs the engine's points plus the profile's Points for each Engineer.

Composition

Closed Order units list their allowed trays as Ranks of Files, for example “4 Ranks of 6 Warriors”. Open Order units list a number of models, for example “4–8 Cavalry”. Chariots and Elephants also list the crew and animals on each chariot or elephant.

Equipment, Gear and Options

Equipment must be shown on the models; Gear need not be. Options show their cost per model (or per chariot or elephant), or “free”. Characters are one model each, at the flat cost shown; a unit may take each listed Character once.

Weapon and Armor Tables

Length/Range: a plain number is the weapon's melee Length. A number with inches (”) is a ranged weapon's range. A Throwing Weapon shows both, for example “6 / 8””: Length 6 in Melee, range 8” when thrown. Keywords in bold are explained in the Rules Reference at the back. The Armor table lists each armor or shield the unit can have, with its Armor modifier and Move penalty.

Command Cards

A Command deck is 6 cards. Each card shows how many copies a deck may hold (Limit). Cards printed in an army's section may only go in that army's deck; Generic Command Cards may go in any deck.
